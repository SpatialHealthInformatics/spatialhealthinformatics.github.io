Spatial Health Informatics

Using spatial statistics, public health surveillance data, and interactive tools to understand where health risks concentrate and how the places people live shape their health.

Focus areas

Spatial statistics

Clustering and hotspot methods such as Moran's I, LISA, and Getis-Ord Gi* to find where outcomes concentrate.

Housing and health equity

How housing conditions, environmental burden, and social vulnerability relate to health across neighborhoods.

Surveillance and decision support

Turning public health surveillance data into maps and dashboards that support decisions.

Interactive visualization

Web-based charts and maps built in Python and ArcGIS so findings can be explored, not only read.

Projects

Housing, Environment, and Health Across Three Cities In development

A comparison of Richmond, California; Cambridge, United Kingdom; and Singapore using open housing, environmental, and health data, applying the same spatial and interactive methods across very different urban settings.

Spatial statistics · Housing indicators · Comparative urban health

Asthma and Environmental Justice in Contra Costa County

Spatial analysis of asthma prevalence against PM2.5, NO2, and the CDC/ATSDR Environmental Justice Index. Asthma showed significant spatial clustering (global Moran's I = 0.31, p < 0.01), with high-high clusters along the Richmond and Pittsburg corridor.

Poisson regression · Global Moran's I · LISA cluster analysis
