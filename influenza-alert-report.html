A scheduled R script downloads the latest weekly trend file, which holds 10 years of national reports per sentinel site. The EARS C2 method compares each week with a 7-week baseline that ends 2 weeks earlier. The score is the number of standard deviations above the baseline mean, with a minimum standard deviation of 0.1 to avoid alerts on very low summer values. A score of 3 or higher is an alert. The season runs from week 36 to week 35 of the next year, and the epidemic threshold is 1 report per sentinel site.

About this report

  • Data source: Japan Institute for Health Security, IDWR provisional data. National series: bundled file. Prefecture values: bundled week 41 sentinel file (teiten). Public, no credentials.
  • Limits: Sentinel site numbers changed from 2025 week 15, when ARI surveillance began, which affects baselines that span that change. IDWR data are provisional and published about 9 days after the week ends.
